# LOOM v0.1 participant worksheet

This is a privacy-safe template for 12–20 Mac design partners. Use a pseudonymous ID such as
`P01`; keep the mapping and any consent records outside this repository. Do not record raw source text,
screenshots, URLs, credentials, raw queries, or private documents here.

## First-session activation

|Participant ID|Unaided correct-source recovery in first session|Matching evidence opened in first session|Activated|Data withdrawn|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|P__|yes / no / not run|yes / no / not run|yes / no / not measured|yes / no|

Activated means setup completed **and** at least one unaided known-item recovery with matching
source evidence opened during the first session. Aggregate activation is activated participants /
consented participants with retained study data who start setup; include setup/retrieval failures.
Before the recorded report cutoff, an explicit consent withdrawal or study-data deletion request
removes that participant from **both numerator and denominator** and excludes all their measurements;
disclose the withdrawn count separately. A later request invalidates an earlier advance decision
until aggregates and cohort eligibility are recalculated and a superseding decision is recorded.
Advancing requires 12–20 enrolled and at least 12 eligible setup participants remaining. Completion
and repeat use below are distinct metrics. Record the report cutoff with the aggregate decision.

Record failure classes, not content. A task may be marked `not run`, `participant withdrew`, or
`source unavailable` with a short aggregate reason. “Evidence-open success” means the participant
opened the exact original or an explicitly labeled preserved snapshot whose artifact/version/hash
matched the result.

## Negative-query metrics

|Participant ID|Negative queries issued|Negative queries completed|Correct no-result disclosures|Unsupported-result failures|Disclosure failures|
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
|P__||||||

Correct disclosure means a completed negative query returned zero unsupported results and explicitly
said no match. Divide correct no-result disclosures by **all completed negative queries**, including
failures; do not use positive known-item tasks as this denominator. Counts must be nonnegative,
completed must not exceed issued, and correct disclosures must not exceed completed. An unsupported
result also triggers the stop rule. A zero denominator for activation or negative queries is
**not measured**, never an invented 0% or 100%, and cannot satisfy the advance gate.
