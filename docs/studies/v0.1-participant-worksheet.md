# LOOM v0.1 participant worksheet

This is a privacy-safe template for 12–20 Mac design partners. Use a pseudonymous ID such as
`P01`; keep the mapping and any consent records outside this repository. Do not record raw source text,
screenshots, URLs, credentials, raw queries, or private documents here.

Record failure classes, not content. A task may be marked `not run`, `participant withdrew`, or
`source unavailable` with a short aggregate reason. “Evidence-open success” means the participant
opened the exact original or an explicitly labeled preserved snapshot whose artifact/version/hash
matched the result.
